========================================================================================================
RECOVERY RATE -- the only number here that can be compared to the outside world
========================================================================================================
  Cycles collected / cycles due is THIS project's metric and it counts
  cycles that never failed. Every published industry figure is a
  RECOVERY RATE: of the payments that failed, the share collected.
  They are different quantities. docs/04_BUILD_PLAN.md W0.

  REVENUE AT RISK is defined by the WORLD, not by any policy: the
  mandate-cycles a debit on the due date would not have covered.
  Identical for every arm, so the arms stay comparable.

                   arm    at risk   recovered   recovery rate   <=10 days   median days
  agent, deterministic       4101        3353          81.80%      35.59%          14.5
    agent, LLM overlay       4101        3274          79.84%      35.56%          14.5

  First-presentation failure rate: 67.60%. A property of the
  WORLD -- no policy moves it. Published figures put real UPI
  AutoPay failure at 8-15% (docs/01_FACTS.md, all [REPORTED],
  all from vendors selling recovery software).

  THE AT-RISK SET EXCLUDES technical declines and the decline
  taxonomy, both properties of the rail rather than of funding.
  That makes it SMALLER than a real failed-payment population and
  so it FLATTERS every recovery rate above. Said here rather than
  in a footnote.

========================================================================================================
THE LLM OVERLAY -- fallback rate, and whether the source changes the outcome
========================================================================================================
  A BOUNDED CALL BUDGET IS THE DESIGN, NOT A WORKAROUND. The loop
  asks for a diagnosis once per live mandate per decision hour --
  tens of thousands of times over this batch. No production agent
  calls a model that often either; it calls one on the novel cases
  and lets rules handle the routine ones. Cache hits are free and
  do not count against the cap, so it bites on NOVELTY, not volume.
  diagnoses refused by the per-run cap : 115217
  diagnoses answered by the model : 8290
  diagnoses that fell back        : 115224
  FALLBACK RATE                   : 93.3%
     115217  per-run live-call cap of 150 reached; routine cases go to th
          7  model returned a payload this layer could not read as a Diag

  DO OUTCOMES DIFFER BY SOURCE? Money is credited by the executor,
  which never sees a `source` field, so this compares like with like.
        source  attempts  succeeded  approval    Rs recovered
           llm       520        328    63.08%      Rs 292,350
      fallback      9390       5361    57.09%    Rs 5,255,240

  A DIFFERENCE HERE IS NOT AN EFFECT OF THE SOURCE. Which cases fall
  back is not random -- a fallback happens on timeout or an
  unparseable reply, and those correlate with case shape. Read this
  as a description of the split, never as a causal comparison.

WHAT THIS NUMBER IS NOT
========================================================================================================
  * No real data. Every figure is simulation; no Razorpay
    transaction, mandate or decline code has ever been seen.
  * Conditional on payday_err=7. At +/-1 day payday_wait wins.
  * The decline taxonomy is OFF here (every rate 0), so this is the
    world without frozen accounts, broken mandates or limit hits.
    With p_limit swept 0.00/0.05/0.15 the cost is
    0.00 / -2.87 / -13.46 pts and every rate is a [GUESS].
  * 8 populations, one run seed each. Not a large study.

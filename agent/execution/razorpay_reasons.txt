# Razorpay `error_reason` values, verbatim.
#
# SOURCE: payments_error_reasons.xlsx, downloaded from razorpay.com on
# 29 August 2026. [VERIFIED], primary source. Recorded in docs/01_FACTS.md.
#
# THIS FILE IS A FIXTURE, NOT A CONFIGURATION. Nothing reads it at runtime.
# `agent/tests/test_razorpay_mapping.py` checks that every reason here has a
# family in `agent/execution/razorpay_codes.py`, so that if Razorpay publishes
# a new reason and someone refreshes this file, the gate goes RED instead of
# the new code silently falling through to AMBIGUOUS. A vocabulary that grows
# without anyone noticing is how a taxonomy quietly stops describing the world.
#
# The spreadsheet lists 114 rows; four are exact duplicates of an earlier row,
# once per payment method. De-duplicated here to 110 distinct values.
#
# Format: reason<TAB>explanation
amount_less_than_minimum_amount	Amount in the payment request is less than the minimum amount. Transacting through some banks have fixed fees. If the payment amount is less than the fixed fee then this error shows up.
authentication_failed	The payment failed as 3D secure, or OTP authentication failed. This could happen if the user cancels the payment on the authentication (OTP submit) screen or enters incorrect authentication details such as OTP.
authorisation_declined_by_psp	PSP app has rejected the authorisation request. This can happen when there is an issue/downtime with the PSP or there's an issue with the customer's VPA.
bank_account_invalid	The bank account is not valid. The customer or bank could have closed the account.
bank_account_validation_failed	The third party validation failed as the given bank account details were incorrect or could not be verified.
bank_cutoff_in_progress	Bank CBS cutoff is in progress. This is a periodic event at the bank's end.
bank_not_available	Bank is not available due to a downtime or a technical issue.
bank_not_enabled	The selected bank to complete the transaction is not enabled for your business.
bank_technical_error	The issuing bank was facing technical problems at the moment the payment was attempted. This usually occurs when the Core Banking System encounters a technical error while processing the payment.
beneficiary_account_does_not_exist	An issue with the beneficiary account which doesn't exist.
beneficiary_account_dormant	An issue with the beneficiary account which is dormant.
capture_failed	Payment was authorized by the bank but the gateway failed to capture it due to some error.
card_declined	Issuer Bank can decline the card due to multiple checks at their end. The exact reason in this case is not shared with Razorpay. Customer needs to reach out to the issuing bank.
card_expired	The customer is making the payment with an expired card.
card_network_not_enabled	The card network through which payment was being attempted is not enabled for your business. For example, payment being tried through Amex credit card and the same is not enabled for your business.
card_not_enrolled	If the card is not enrolled, this means that either the issuing bank (of the card) does not support 3DS or the card holder has not yet registered for the 3D secure service.
card_number_invalid	The customer has entered an incorrect card number which is not part of any BIN/ IIN.
card_type_invalid	The customer is using an unsupported card type to complete the payment. For example, mutual fund purchases are not allowed via credit cards.
collect_on_mcc_blocked	NPCI blocks collect requests on certain MCCs. (6540).
collect_request_pending	Collect Request is pending. Customer has not actioned on it or the callback is not yet received by the acquirer.
compliance_violation	Any compliance violation at the customer or merchant level.
credit_limit_exceeded	The customer has exceeded the credit limit extended to him. This error occurs in Cardless EMI payments.
credit_limit_expired	The credit limit for the customer has expired. This error occurs in Cardless EMI payments.
credit_limit_inactive	The credit limit for the customer is inactive. This may be due to pending action from the customer's end. This error occurs in Cardless EMI payments.
credit_limit_not_approved	The credit limit for the customer is not approved. This error occurs in Cardless EMI payments.
credit_not_permitted	The beneficiary bank has not allowed the credit to happen into the merchant's account. This can be because of the TPV account mismatch or an issue at the beneficiary bank.
credit_failed	The beneficiary bank has not allowed the credit to happen into the merchant's account. This can be because of TPV account mismatch or an issue at the beneficiary bank.
debit_declined	The customer bank has declined the debit request. One of the reasons can be because of the account being blocked.
deemed_transaction	This is the case of a deemed transaction wherein the status of the transaction is not known to the acquirer and the status will be known on the next day.
debit_instrument_blocked	The customer is using a blocked card to complete the payment. The card could have been blocked by the issuer or by customers themselves.
debit_instrument_inactive	The customer is using an inactive or frozen card to complete the payment. The card could have been marked inactive by the issuer or by customer themselves.
duplicate_refund_id	Two refunds with the same Refund ID have been initiated.
duplicate_request	A payment initiation request with the exact same parameters was passed to the gateway. This can be an integration issue with the merchant.
duplicate_rrn_found	A payment with the same RRN is found. A very rare case but possible as the RRN is not unique. This is a rare and temporary issue which can be fixed by a retry.
emi_greater_than_max_amount	The EMI amount is greater than the maximum amount allowed for the customer. This error occurs in Cardless EMI payments.
emi_plan_unavailable	The customer-selected EMI plan is no longer supported by the EMI provider. This error occurs in Cardless EMI payments.
funds_blocked_by_mandate	This is an error for One time Mandate product. Funds are blocked and customer is trying to access these funds via payment.
gateway_technical_error	Payment failed due to a technical error at the gateway. This usually occurs when the gateway server encounters a technical error while processing the payment.
incorrect_atm_pin	Some banks ask for Debit card information for authentication. If the customer enters the ATM pin incorrectly, this error is thrown during the UPI registeration process. Note: This is a very rare error code in payment transactions.
incorrect_card_details	This is a generic error message when a customer has entered incorrect card details to complete the payment. The customer has to enter multiple values like cardholder name, expiry date, cvv to complete the payment.
incorrect_card_expiry_date	The customer has entered an incorrect expiry date of the card.
incorrect_cardholder_name	The customer has entered an incorrect name of the card holder.
incorrect_cvv	The customer has entered an incorrect CVV to complete the payment.
incorrect_otp	The customer has entered an incorrect OTP to complete the payment.
incorrect_pin	The customer has entered an incorrect PIN to complete the payment.
input_validation_failed	Payment failed due to wrong request or input sent in the payment request. This is also seen while creating a payment with incorrect parameter values on the Dashboard.
insufficient_funds	The customer does not have sufficient funds in the account to complete the payment.
international_transaction_not_allowed	International transactions are not enabled for your account or for a specific product. Refer to the error description parameter for more details.
invalid_amount	Amount or currency passed in the payment request is not supported or invalid. This can arise when you pass a different variable type in the amount field or pass an unsupported amount value.
invalid_currency	The currency type for the request you are sending is not enabled or not supported for your business. For example, you are attempting a payment in British pound when international currency is not enabled for your account.
invalid_device	UPI mandates that all transactions must at least be 2FA using a mobile device(the device fingerprint) as one factor and the UPI PIN as the second. This error occurs when a user tries to complete the payment using an unregistered device to send or receive payments using that account.
invalid_email	The customer has provided an invalid email.
invalid_mobile_number	The customer used an unregistered or invalid mobile number to complete the transaction. This error occurs in Wallet payments.
invalid_order_id	Order ID required in the payment request is either missing or is invalid. Order ID is mandatory for every payment.
invalid_response_from_gateway	The response received from the gateway is invalid.
invalid_request	The request is invalid because of the information that the merchant has passed.
invalid_user_details	The customer does not exist.
invalid_vpa	The customer is using an invalid VPA or an unregistered VPA to complete the payment.
issuer_technical_error	Payment failed due to a technical error at the issuer. This usually occurs when the gateway server encounters a technical error while processing the payment.
live_mode_not_enabled	Live mode is not enabled for your business. This error generally arises when you use test mode keys to make a live payment.
mandate_creation_declined	Mandate creation failed. This is applicable for UPI Autopay and UPI OTM.
mandate_creation_expired	Mandate creation expired.
mandate_creation_failed	Mandate creation declined by an entity.
mandate_creation_timeout	Mandate creation timed out.
mcc_amount_limit_exceeded	NPCI restricts Collect requests over 5k for some MCCs (4814 & 4812).
merchant_not_activated	Merchant is not activated with the gateway. An issue with the merchant's terminal/account.
mismatch_in_transaction_details	The merchant has not passed the transaction details correctly.
mobile_number_invalid	The customer is using an invalid or an unregistered mobile number to complete the payment. This means that the mobile number is not mapped to the bank account.
order_already_paid	There can only be one successful payment for each order ID. This error arises when you are trying to use an order ID where a payment is already completed.
order_payment_method_mismatch	This error arises when the method mentioned in the order request is different from the method mentioned in the payment request.
order_amount_mismatch	This error arises when the amount mentioned in the order request is different from the amount mentioned in the payment request.
otp_attempts_exceeded	The customer has entered the wrong OTP multiple times and exceeded the limit. Some issuers limit the number of OTP retries, beyond which the card is temporarily blocked.
otp_expired	The customer has entered an expired OTP.
payment_amount_tampered	The payment amount has been tampered.
payment_cancelled	The customer has explicitly cancelled the payment due to which the authentication failed to complete.
payment_collect_request_expired	The UPI collect request time period has expired. For example, in most collect requests the expiry period is 5-10 minutes within the payment has to be completed by the customer on the UPI app. If a customer fails to complete the payment during this time, the collect request is marked as expired and the payment fails.
payment_declined	Issuer Bank or Gateway has declined the payment due to business or technical reasons. The exact reason in this case is not communicated to Razorpay.
payment_declined_due_to_high_traffic	Primarily occurs in the case of high TPS scenarios like IPL wherein the bank is unable to serve requests.
payment_failed	Payment processing failed due to error at bank or wallet gateway. No specific error code received from gateway in this case.
payment_method_not_enabled	The selected payment method is not enabled for your business. This error occurs when your customer tries to complete a transaction using a method that is not enabled for you.
payment_pending	The payment was marked as pending by the bank or the gateway. Pending transactions may later on become authorized. This is known as late authorization.
payment_pending_approval	The payment is currently pending approval by the concerned authority as part of the maker-checker flow.
payment_risk_check_failed	Payment declined due to risk checks. Risk checks are performed by Razorpay, Gateway and Issuer Bank. The source parameter would give additional clarity where the risk check failed.
payment_session_expired	The payment session expired as the payment was not completed within the time by the customer. This is mostly due to an inactive payment window.
payment_timed_out	The customer did not complete the transaction within the specified time. This error may also happen when no response is received from the gateway.
pin_attempts_exceeded	The customer has entered the wrong PIN multiple times and exceeded the limit. Some issuers limit the maximum number of PIN retries beyond which the card is temporarily blocked.
pin_not_set	There is no PIN set by the customer for the UPI account.
psp_app_ not_available	PSP app is not available. This can be because of a downtime with the PSP.
psp_app_not_supported	UPI App is not supported. This is a rare error used when a particular app is blacklisted.
psp_not_available	PSP is not available due to a downtime at their end.
psp_not_registered	The PSP is not registered on the customer's device.
record_not_found	A record of a payment is not found at the bank. This usually happens when a status check call is hit for intent payments for which the status check call is not hit.
recurring_payment_not_enabled	This error arises when you are trying to process a recurring payment when recurring payments are not enabled for your business.
refund_limit_crossed	Refund amount limit crossed.
reqauth_mandate_not_acknowledged	PSP did not respond to the authorisation request for the mandate. This can happen due to a downtime with a PSP.
request_timed_out	The request timed out.
server_error	Technical error at Razorpay's server. This usually occurs when there is some server issue at Razorpay's end.
transaction_daily_count_exceeded	The customer has exceeded the maximum number of transactions that can be performed daily for his card.
transaction_daily_limit_exceeded	The customer has exceeded the daily transaction limit set on the card. Some of the cards allow customers to set a limit or have a default limit set.
transaction_limit_exceeded	The customers have exceeded the credit or debit limit set on their cards. This error usually arises while doing high value transactions.
transaction_frequency_limit_exceeded	NPCI has a transaction limit both on the amount and the frequency per day. Customer has exceeded this limit.
transaction_on_vpa_restricted	Transaction on this VPA has been temporarily/permanently blocked by the PSP.
upi_app_technical_error	Technical error occurred at the customer's PSP due to which the payment failed.
upi_autopay_not_supported_on_psp	UPI Autopay is not supported on PSP.
upi_collect_not_enabled	UPI Collect flow is not enabled however merchant has hit the Collect payment request on API.
upi_intent_not_enabled	UPI Intent flow is not enabled however merchant has hit the Intent Payment request on API.
user_not_eligible	The customer failed the eligibility check and is not eligible for credit. This error occurs in Cardless EMI payments.
user_not_registered_for_netbanking	The customer's bank account is not registered for netbanking.
vpa_resolution_failed	The UPI network failed to validate the VPA. This is a technical error when the resolution service of the NPCI fails.
verification_failed	Verification of the payment using the status check API has failed.
